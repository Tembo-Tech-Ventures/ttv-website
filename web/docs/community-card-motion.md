# Community card motion

The reusable design workflow is [ttv-ui-motion](../../.agents/skills/ttv-ui-motion/SKILL.md).
It was independently forward-tested by a local subagent and revised before use.
The homepage source, candidate history in `homepage.md`, and rendered desktop/
mobile homepage establish the visual reference: tangible sheets, a connected
community, punchy balanced type, deep green and warm peach.

## Applied brief

Supporting sheets gather around a contribution file, make contact, and square
onto the page. Their silhouette changes from a separated, oblique arrangement
into three upright surfaces with identical dimensions and corner geometry.
The main gesture is the larger foreground approach from the viewer's side of
the screen. Quiet hero typography anchors the composition. Foreground content
stays attached to its containing surface, with no independent row entrances.

One existing connector leads attention from the middle evidence item into the
last contextual panel: the member's contribution to a mentor's scoped note;
the mentor's recorded observation to its scope; the institution's distinct
sources to a human question. Nodes do not activate or change status. This is
shared file assembly with content-specific reading emphasis, not three unrelated
performances. Rear motifs use the existing dot/ring/diamond vocabulary.

Research principle: changing relationships and content continuity give motion
meaning. The treatment borrows that reasoning from Goodgrowth and Podium, not
their console aesthetic, spiral loader, or film transitions. No new dependency,
interaction, verification status, or content promise is introduced.

Reject the treatment if the sheets read as unrelated panels on equal timers,
if they look rubbery, if borders cross foreground copy, or if the final gesture
suggests an automated approval. Complete the main approach promptly; mobile
reduces depth and spread, and reduced motion shows the final file immediately.

## Review and iteration

Local recordings cover members, mentors, and institutions at desktop and phone
sizes. The first iteration showed rear artwork ghosting through a transparent
foreground and depth resolving too quickly. The second makes the front opaque
earlier and gives the approach more visible travel. Independent frame-based
critique of that iteration found coherent material, containment and homepage
fit, but a weak final handoff, an unnecessary settling tail, schematic motifs,
and an inconsistent mentor-ring color.

The third removes the additional underline phrase, strengthens just one existing
connector, shortens the whole phrase to about 1.13 seconds, simplifies the rear
motifs and restores the teal mentor ring. Video-derived frames supplement timed
browser captures; frame review has limits in judging continuous cadence.
Aesthetic conclusions are judgments, not automated test results or award claims.

Lifecycle coverage includes normal completion, occlusion by sticky sheets,
fast-scroll interruption, preference changes, failed enhancement and late fonts.
Finished animations release their styles and observers. A bounded fallback
reveals server content; late readiness cannot hide that content again.
