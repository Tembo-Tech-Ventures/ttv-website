# Creative reasoning from motion research

These are precedents and interpretive lessons, not a recipe or award guarantee.
Revisit the source or current live experience before making a specific claim.

- [Siena Film Foundation](https://www.awwwards.com/siena-film-foundation-case-study.html):
  filmstrips, tickets, lettering, architectural stripes, and interaction-speed
  blur share a cinematic premise. Repeated visual logic gives effects identity.
- [Goodgrowth](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2026/08/27/goodgrowth-boot-sequences-spinning-discs-and-the-art-of-the-portfolio/):
  console nostalgia governs the experience. Thumbnail gathering combines
  accelerating angle with shrinking radius and a trailing stagger. Changing
  relationships create a developing gesture, beyond repeating one tween.
- [Podium](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2026/06/23/podium-building-a-website-where-running-becomes-storytelling/):
  selected media grows into the next page. An elaborate pixel transition was
  removed because it competed with the films. Continuity and subtraction can
  strengthen a composition more than an additional effect.
- [Adrien Vanderpotte](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2026/05/27/whooshes-snaps-and-shaders-adrien-vanderpotte-and-the-feeling-of-the-interface/):
  Telescope uses optical behavior; Sandy Shore uses a narrative of growth.
  Describe the feeling and material behavior before picking tools.
- [Naked City Films](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2026/01/19/naked-city-films-designing-and-building-a-website-that-refuses-to-stand-still/):
  decisive motion and editorial cuts fit a controlled identity. Intense
  movement needs quiet surroundings and strong graphic composition.
- [Disney animation](https://www.disneyanimation.com/process/animation/) and
  [layout](https://www.disneyanimation.com/process/layout/): anticipation,
  follow-through, weight, staging, camera and framing shape a performance.
  Application to interfaces is an analogy, not a demand to simulate physics.
- [Apple, Designing Fluid Interfaces](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2018/803/):
  continuous response, momentum, interruption, and spatial consistency connect
  an interface to input. Overshoot is purposeful, not a default.
- [Awwwards criteria](https://www.awwwards.com/about-evaluation/): design,
  usability, creativity, and content are considered together. Complexity and
  technically smooth recordings are not independent quality measures.

Questions that expose an underdeveloped idea:

- Would this movement make equal sense transplanted onto any SaaS page?
- What do I look at first, and what invites my eye to move next?
- What does the object feel made of? Does arrival/departure agree with that?
- Where does tension build and release? Is the pause meaningful or just delay?
- Does the interaction preserve the visitor's agency and reading pace?
- Which effect can be removed to make the central gesture clearer?
